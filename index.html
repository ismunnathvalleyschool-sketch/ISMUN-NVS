Message from the Director

Mr. Ranjit Dass · Director

Dear Delegates,

A warm welcome to all of you to the 5th edition of ISMUN. In today’s world the voice of the youth resonates a lot in the Universe and has begun to shape the future. We can see it happening not only in India, but as I write this, in Spain and France too. In many areas society and people are suffering from inequality, hunger and disease. To make matters worse we have dictatorial leadership in the major powers who thrive on war; live examples are before us in the Middle East and Ukraine.

I hope all of you will deliberate all these issues and resolve to influence world leaders to make a safer, healthier and happier world.

Ranjit Dass
Director

Message from the Principal

Dr. Swarup Dutta · Principal

Dear Delegates,

Welcome to ISMUN 4.0!

At Nath Valley School, we envision education as a journey that transcends the classroom, cultivating not only academic distinction but also intellectual depth, global consciousness, and a nuanced understanding of the world. ISMUN 4.0 embodies this vision, offering our students a compelling platform to engage with the intricacies of international diplomacy, interrogate diverse perspectives, articulate their convictions, and experience the profound power of dialogue.

As you deliberate, negotiate, and collaborate, remember that true leadership resides not merely in the eloquence of argument, but in the grace of open-mindedness, the wisdom to reconcile differences, and the resolve to forge meaningful solutions.

May this endeavour inspire you to emerge as discerning thinkers, empathetic leaders, and conscientious global citizens, committed to shaping a more just, inclusive, and harmonious world.

Wishing you an intellectually enriching and truly memorable ISMUN 4.0!

Dr Swarup Dutta
Principal
Nath Valley School

Message from the MUN Coordinator

Mrs. Chaitali Shetty · MUN Coordinator

Dear Students,

As you gear up for our Intra-School MUN 5.0, I want each one of you to remember that this experience is much more than a conference or a debate. It is an opportunity to discover your voice, challenge your thinking, and learn something new about the world and yourselves.

Whether this is your first MUN or you have participated before, come with an open mind, a willingness to learn, and the confidence to express your ideas. Do not worry about making mistakes or having all the answers. What matters is your willingness to listen, ask questions, respect different opinions, and find solutions together.

Step out of your comfort zones, support one another, and truly enjoy every moment of the experience. Celebrate the small victories, learn from the challenges, and appreciate the diverse perspectives your fellow delegates bring to the table.

Remember, the real success of MUN is not measured by the number of awards you win, but by how much you learn, how much you grow, and the memories you create along the way.

Participate with confidence, debate with respect, learn with curiosity, and enjoy every moment. Give it your best, and let this MUN be a memorable learning experience for you!

Warm wishes,
Mrs. Chaitali Shetty
MUN Coordinator

Message from the Secretary-General

Jahnvi Chechani · Secretary-General

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the 4th Edition of the Nath Valley Intra-School Model United Nations (ISMUN 4.0). As we embark on yet another edition of this conference, I am filled with pride and anticipation for the conversations, collaborations, and ideas that will unfold over these two days.

Model United Nations is much more than a platform for debate. It is an experience that teaches us to listen before we speak, understand before we judge, and collaborate despite our differences. It brings together individuals with diverse perspectives, encouraging them to step beyond the boundaries of their own understanding and engage with the complexities of the world.

As delegates, I encourage you to approach every discussion with an open mind, every disagreement with respect, and every challenge with an eagerness to learn. Do not measure your experience solely by arguments you win, but by the perspectives you gain, the ideas you exchange, and the person you become through the process.

To all our first-time delegates, I hope you know that you belong here just as much as anyone else. Your first speech need not be perfect, your arguments need not be flawless, and you certainly do not need to have all the answers.

Delegates, embrace the unfamiliar, ask questions without hesitation, and allow yourself the freedom to learn, make mistakes, and grow. Discover what you are capable of becoming.

Beyond the formalities of debate, I hope you find friendships in unexpected places, confidence in moments of uncertainty, and inspiration in conversations that challenge the way you think. When these two days come to an end, may the memories you create remain long after the placards are put away.

I wish all delegates a rewarding and memorable conference.

Until October,

Jahnvi Chechani
Secretary-General
